I wanted to refresh my portfolio and give the Juana y Lola Digital website
a more professional look. I used [Impeccable](https://impeccable.style/)
alongside Codex to do it. What surprised me most was how straightforward
the process felt and how good the results were.

Impeccable brings design guidance to working with AI agents, helping with
decisions about typography, spacing, visual hierarchy, and consistency.
For me, it was a practical way to give the redesign direction while
working with Codex, without having to turn every request into a detailed
CSS specification.

## A portfolio with its own identity

On [my personal website](https://sebastiancastaneda.dev/), the change was a
complete redesign. I moved from a layout with sidebar navigation and
yellow accents to a terminal aesthetic, with monospace headings, green
tones, and navigation at the top. The result feels closer to my work as an
engineer and gives my experience and projects a clearer visual identity.

I also used this combination to improve
[Juana y Lola Digital](https://juanaylola.com/). There, I wanted a more
professional presentation for the development and consulting services.
The two websites serve different purposes, and I liked being able to work
on both without having them end up looking like the same template.

## The details that make a difference

What I value most is having removed those visual artifacts that often
give away an AI-generated interface: inconsistent spacing, elements
competing for attention, and decoration that adds little. Across these
projects, the result feels more coherent and considered.

That combination of ease of use and visual quality is what stays with me.
Impeccable helped me turn ideas into a result I am happy with, while
keeping a distinct personality for each website. If you already work with
Codex and want to improve your projects' design, my experience with this
combination has been very positive.
