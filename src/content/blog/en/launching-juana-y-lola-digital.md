I'm launching [Juana y Lola Digital](https://juanaylola.com/), an initiative to
provide software development and IT consulting services to small local
businesses and entrepreneurs.

After more than ten years working in software engineering, I want to bring
that experience closer to people building and running their own businesses.

## What I want to help with

Every business has its own way of working. Choosing software, building a
website, or figuring out how technology can support day-to-day work can take
time away from running the business.

Through Juana y Lola Digital, I want to help with:

- **Software development:** building websites and software around the needs
  of a business.
- **IT consulting:** understanding a problem, exploring the available options,
  and choosing a practical way forward.

My starting point is a conversation about what the business needs. From
there, the goal is to build something useful and maintainable.

## The website

The Juana y Lola Digital website is built with Astro and hosted on Netlify.
I use Codex as part of my development workflow.

## Let's talk

If you run a small business or are starting a new venture and need help
with software or IT, I'd love to hear what you're working on.

Visit [juanaylola.com](https://juanaylola.com/) to learn more and get in touch.
